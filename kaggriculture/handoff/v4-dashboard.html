Fixes ledger — what moved 71k → 124k today

Every item verified against engine source or replay data before shipping.

  1. Concurrent animal ritual. The old dispatcher gave each animal one task per turn, care last at priority 40 — coverage measured 6–12 of 14. Now feed, care, collect and harvest are emitted together; coverage is 14/14 from day 16. Engine: each fed+cared day banks +1 product; an unfed production day zeroes the whole bank (kaggriculture.py:796-800).
  2. Feed outranks care (90 > 85). First attempt inverted it and two animals escaped; a cared-but-unfed animal wastes the op and walks toward escape.
  3. Animal census counted carried animals. Before: 10 cows + 12 sheep bought for targets of 8 + 6; the surplus cows squatted pastures and 8 sheep ($4,000) died in the shed.
  4. Feed-wheat accounting. Reserve checks now see shed + hands; a shed-only check re-bought wheat every turn until cash pinned at the $150 floor (episode collapsed to 10k — caught locally).
  5. Strawberry 16 → 44 tiles, ramp from day 6, planting window to day 17, seed drip widened. 66 → 236 units.
  6. FERTILIZE exists now. Strawberries get fertilized (+1 unit per watered production day, worth ~$120–180 vs selling the fertilizer at ~$100); 79 applications and climbing.
  7. Melon cut 16 → 13 tiles, no replant past day 13. Melon's quadratic glut curve punishes volume; the tuner confirmed +5,973 for the cut across 16 paired games.
  8. Third quadrant by day 12. The 6-empty-tiles trigger fired one day after the purchase window closed; farm sat on 2 quadrants with $10–30k idle. Threshold raised to 18.
  9. Herd front-loaded. Cash reserve 2,500 → 900, cow-led round-robin, 2 cows + 1 sheep on day 0 — matching the clones' 4-animals-by-day-3 opening.
